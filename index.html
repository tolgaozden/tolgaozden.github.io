Tolga Ozden

I am an economist in the Macroeconomics Research Division of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) in Manila. I work on macroeconomic modeling, forecasting and scenario analysis.

Before joining ADB, I was an Assistant Director at the Bank of Canada, where I led the development of the Bank's new macroeconomic projection model and forecast scenarios around inflation risks. I hold a PhD in economics from the University of Amsterdam (2021).

My research spans macroeconomics and time series econometrics, with current work on the macroeconomics of inequality and of climate events such as El Niño.
