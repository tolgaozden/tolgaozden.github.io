HetLearn Toolbox

HetLearn Toolbox

A MATLAB toolbox for forecasts and policy scenarios in DSGE models where not everyone has rational expectations.

Open the documentationGitHub repository

What it does

hetlearn is built on the IRIS Toolbox. Expectations of chosen forward-looking variables are a mix of rational-expectations agents and adaptive learners, who forecast from a simple perceived law of motion and update it by recursive least squares. The shares of the two groups switch each quarter with their recent forecast errors, following Ozden (2025, International Journal of Central Banking).

You bring a linear IRIS model file and a database. The toolbox writes the heterogeneous-expectations version of the model, learns beliefs and shares over the history, and runs your scenario forward one quarter at a time. The documentation walks through worked examples on the Smets and Wouters (2007) model with US data.
